# Kill James Bond! vs The Bechdel Cast — essays

**Read it here:** https://liaminhawai-cmd.github.io/Linguistics-essays/

An unfinished comparative discourse study of two feminist film podcasts, *Kill James Bond!* and *The Bechdel Cast*, working film by film through the episodes both shows made. There are eleven essays and four synthesis chapters, plus the running findings ledger and the analytic brief that governs the study.

## What's here

- `essays/` and `synthesis/` — the chapters, as Markdown. The site renders them with `essay.html`.
- `FINDINGS.md` — the running scoreboard: what has held up, what was corrected, and what's still open.
- `ANALYTIC_BRIEF.md` — the study's rules and working hypotheses.
- `tagging/` — the fixed word lists, the counting script, and the per-episode counts (`counts.json`) behind every number in the essays.

## What isn't

The episode transcripts, the reference podcasts used as lenses, and the third-party review texts live in a separate private repository. They are other people's work, so they aren't republished here. The counts in `tagging/counts.json` were generated from them, and `tagging/count.py` reproduces them for anyone with the transcripts.

One paragraph is left out of the public copy of the brief: a hypothesis about the hosts' biographies. The study keeps that out of scope and says nothing about anyone's private life or medical history beyond their own public statements.
